# Proposal: Behavior Tracker

1. **Choose and frame the problem.** What problem, project, or workflow are you most interested in working on, and why? What makes it worth exploring?

2. **Design the solution.** How would you use AI - tools, workflows, agents, or integrations - to approach the problem? Show us the thinking, not just the tools.

3. **Prove it, if you can.** If you're able to, build something - a prototype, a working script, a prompt system, an agent workflow, or any artefact that demonstrates your approach. If you can't build it in the time available, show us the blueprint and walk us through what you'd do with more runway.

4. **Analyze the impact.** Compare your approach before and after using AI. What improved? What became faster, clearer, cheaper, more ambitious, or more possible? Where did AI augmentation break down? What still required human judgment? What would you need to learn to take this further?
